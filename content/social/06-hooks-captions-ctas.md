# Hooks, captions & CTAs

## Hook formulas that fit you

1. **Contrarian local:** “People ask me if Fountain Square is worth it…”  
2. **Operator truth:** “The spreadsheet never shows turnover day.”  
3. **Identity:** “I’m an agent, investor, and host — those three jobs argue until the answer gets better.”  
4. **Specific place:** “Morning at Riu House in Paquera…”  
5. **Offer help:** “Send me 5 listing photos…”  
6. **Clear filter:** “If you want TikTok math, keep scrolling.”

Open with the line. Don’t warm up.

## Caption skeletons

### Buyer
```
Most buyers fall in love with paint.

I fall in love with layout, light, and whether this place still works when life changes.

Buying in Indianapolis or looking at Costa Rica?
Message me — tell me how you actually live.
```

### Seller
```
I prep homes like a guest is checking in tomorrow.

Calm photos. Clear surfaces. A life someone can picture.

Selling soon? Send 5 phone photos and I’ll tell you what’s helping vs hurting.
```

### Investor
```
I don’t sell fantasy returns.

I ask: realistic occupancy, real expenses, who operates it, and what’s the exit.

Investing in Indy stays or Costa Rica hospitality?
DM me which market — I’ll share the first questions I’d ask.
```

### Stay / brand
```
Stay Different. Stay NuvoHauz.

Thoughtfully designed homes in Fountain Square and Paquera — hosted like it matters.

Book direct from the link in bio, or WhatsApp me if you have questions.
```

## On-screen text (keep tiny)

- 3–6 words max per flash  
- Match the spoken hook  
- End card examples: `Buyers → DM me` · `Investors → INDY or CR` · `Stay Different · NuvoHauz`

## Music / audio

- Soft tropical acoustic or clean lo-fi for CR beauty cuts  
- Low ambient / no vocal competition when you’re teaching  
- Original audio when the hook is the point — algorithm likes it, and it sounds like you

## DM reply snippets (when leads come in)

**Buyer:** “Hey — tell me Indy or Costa Rica, budget range if you’re comfortable, and must-haves. I’ll tell you if I can actually help.”  

**Seller:** “Thanks for the photos — here’s what I’d change first: [1][2][3]. Want a straight pricing conversation next?”  

**Investor:** “Got it — Indy or CR? Are you planning to self-manage or hire it out? That changes everything.”  

**Stay:** “Happy to help — dates and how many guests? I can check Riu House / the collection or point you to the right stay.”
