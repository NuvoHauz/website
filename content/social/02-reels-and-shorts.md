# Reels & Shorts — ready-to-film scripts

Format for each: **Hook (0–2s)** → **Body** → **Close/CTA** · Target length **20–35s** unless noted.

Speak these in your own words. Numbers in brackets = swap with your real ones.

---

## BUYERS

### B1 — “I buy homes like I’ll host them”
**Hook:** “I don’t look at houses the way most buyers do.”  
**Body:** “I’m thinking: Where does the suitcase go? Can two couples sleep without sharing a bathroom? Is the kitchen actually usable after a long travel day?”  
**B-roll:** Walk through Banksy or Carriage House — closet, bathroom door, kitchen island.  
**Close:** “If you’re buying a primary or a future rental, I’ll help you shop with host eyes. Buyers — message me.”

### B2 — Fountain Square walkability (Indy)
**Hook:** “People ask me if Fountain Square is ‘worth it.’”  
**Body:** “Here’s the real answer: if you want art, food, and downtown without living in a glass box — yes. If you want silence and a big yard — maybe not this block.”  
**B-roll:** Street art, porch, sidewalk, downtown skyline from a distance.  
**Close:** “Tell me how you actually live. I’ll tell you if this neighborhood fits.”

### B3 — Costa Rica buyer reality check
**Hook:** “Buying in Costa Rica isn’t hard. Buying *well* is.”  
**Body:** “Ask about water, internet, road access in rainy season, and who manages the place when you’re not there. Pretty photos don’t pay for repairs.”  
**B-roll:** Drive into Paquera, terrace rain, Wi‑Fi router, property gate.  
**Close:** “If you’re serious about Nicoya, I’ll tell you what I’d verify before you wire a dollar.”

### B4 — First-time buyer, no fluff
**Hook:** “First home? Cool. Don’t buy the Instagram version.”  
**Body:** “Buy the boring stuff: roof, HVAC, layout, resale. Pretty paint is cheap. Bad bones are expensive.”  
**Close:** “DM me ‘FIRST’ and I’ll send the 7 things I check before I get excited.”

## SELLERS

### S1 — Staging like a host
**Hook:** “I stage like guests are checking in tomorrow.”  
**Body:** “Clear counters. Soft light. One hero photo angle — not forty cluttered ones. Buyers feel calm or they feel overwhelmed in three seconds.”  
**B-roll:** Before/after counter, made bed, open curtains.  
**Close:** “Selling in Indy or listing a vacation home? I’ll help you present it the way guests already respond to.”

### S2 — Why empty houses underperform
**Hook:** “An empty house photographs cold.”  
**Body:** “A lived-in, edited house photographs like a life someone wants. That’s the difference between ‘nice listing’ and ‘I can see myself here.’”  
**Close:** “If you’re prepping to sell, send me 5 phone photos. I’ll tell you what’s helping and what’s hurting — free.”

### S3 — Honest pricing talk
**Hook:** “Overpricing isn’t confidence. It’s a delay.”  
**Body:** “I’d rather put you at the number that brings the right buyers in week one than watch you chase the market down for two months.”  
**Close:** “Sellers who want a straight conversation — not a pep talk — message me.”

## INVESTORS

### I1 — Your unique wedge
**Hook:** “I’m not a theorist. I have keys.”  
**Body:** “Indianapolis short-term stays. Costa Rica hospitality. Same question every time: will a real guest leave a review that fills the next calendar?”  
**B-roll:** Key in lock, calendar/phone, pool, Fountain Square exterior.  
**Close:** “Investors who want operator-level advice — not TikTok math — talk to me.”

### I2 — Indy vs Costa Rica (honest)
**Hook:** “Indy and Costa Rica are not the same investment.”  
**Body:** “Indy: closer management, clearer comps, different guest profile. Costa Rica: lifestyle + tourism demand, more logistics, more local partnership needed. Pick the problem you want.”  
**Close:** “Comment INDY or CR and I’ll tell you which questions I’d ask first for that market.”

### I3 — STR myth bust
**Hook:** “Nobody tells you about turnover day.”  
**Body:** “The spreadsheet shows ADR. Real life shows: laundry, restocking, a late checkout, a missing remote. If you hate operations, design the deal for a manager — or don’t buy a STR.”  
**B-roll:** Towels, cleaning caddy, thermostat, door code pad.  
**Close:** “Want a deal that fits how you actually live? Investors — DM me.”

### I4 — What made NuvoHauz work
**Hook:** “NuvoHauz didn’t start as a ‘portfolio.’”  
**Body:** “It started as one home done carefully — design, guest experience, then another. Growth followed the standard, not the other way around.”  
**B-roll:** Riu House exterior/pool, Morpho/Casita, Banksy House.  
**Close:** “If you want to build stays people remember, not just list doors — let’s talk.”

### I5 — Numbers without flexing (25–40s)
**Hook:** “Here’s how I evaluate a stay property — simple.”  
**Body:** “1) Realistic occupancy, not best month. 2) All-in expenses — cleaning, software, supplies, maintenance. 3) Time cost if I’m the operator. 4) Exit: who buys this if rules change?”  
**Close:** “Send me an address you’re considering. I’ll tell you what I’d pressure-test.”
