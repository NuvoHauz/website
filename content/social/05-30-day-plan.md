# 30-day posting plan

Cadence: **5–6 posts/week** (Reels/Shorts primary · 2 carousels/week · Stories daily when you can).  
Mix rule: never more than 2 investor posts in a row — rotate buyer / seller / investor / brand.

Swap days if you’re traveling. Prefer real footage from that week over a perfect plan.

## Platform notes

- **Instagram Reels + TikTok:** same video, slightly different caption length.  
- **YouTube Shorts:** same vertical file; put the CTA spoken + on-screen.  
- **Stories:** daily face + one question; don’t overproduce.  
- **Link in bio:** NuvoHauz site + WhatsApp; pin “Buyers / Sellers / Investors — tell me which.”
