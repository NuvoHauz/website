# Carousels — slide-by-slide copy

Design notes: use your real photos. Serif headline energy like the site. Cream/sand + warm gold (#C69C6D energy) is on-brand — skip purple gradients and neon stickers. Max ~15 words per slide. Last slide = one CTA.

---

## C1 — Buyers: “Host eyes” checklist
**Audience:** buyers · **Title cover:** What I check that most buyers skip

1. Cover: What I check that most buyers skip  
2. Luggage drop zone in the first 10 steps inside  
3. Bathroom count vs. how people actually travel  
4. Kitchen that works after a long day (not just “granite”)  
5. Noise at night — street, neighbors, HVAC  
6. Storage: closets beat cute shelves  
7. Resale / rental optionality if life changes  
8. CTA: Buying in Indy or looking at Costa Rica? Message Sandy.
