# Your voice (so it sounds like you, not AI)

## Who you are on camera

You’re Sandy — the person guests already message on WhatsApp.  
You’re not a hype coach. You’re someone who:

- bought and fixed up homes people actually want to stay in
- hosts guests in Fountain Square and Paquera
- helps buyers, sellers, and investors make clearer decisions because you live both sides of the deal

Tone: warm, direct, design-aware, a little dry humor when it fits. Short sentences. Real numbers when you have them. Admit what you don’t know.

## Say it like this

| Instead of… | Try… |
|---|---|
| “Unlock passive income!” | “This house paid for itself the slow way — stays, not hype.” |
| “Dream home alert 🚨” | “I wouldn’t list this if I wouldn’t host it myself.” |
| “Market is on fire!!!” | “Here’s what buyers in Fountain Square are actually asking me this week.” |
| “Limited spots left!!” | “If this is you, message me. If it’s not, scroll — no hard sell.” |
| “As a real estate professional…” | “I’ve bought here, hosted here, and sold clients into this same neighborhood.” |

## Signature angles only you can own

1. **Two countries, one standard** — Indy design + Costa Rica hospitality, same bar.
2. **Host eyes on every listing** — you notice storage, noise, laundry, check-in flow because guests complain to you about those things.
3. **Investor who still changes sheets** — credibility without bragging.
4. **Paquera honesty** — ferry timing, wildlife, what’s 20 minutes vs. “close to the beach” marketing.
5. **Fountain Square local** — art, walkability, downtown access — not “up-and-coming” clichés.

## Words that fit NuvoHauz

thoughtful · curated · modern · warm · stay different · direct · designed · managed · Nicoya · Fountain Square · river · terrace · pool · guest-ready

## Words to avoid

game-changer · unlock · crush it · passive income empire · motivational bro talk · fake urgency · emoji walls · “DM me ‘READY’” bait · purple-glow aesthetic captions

## On-camera habits

- Look at the lens like you’re texting a smart friend.
- Open with the point in the first 1–2 seconds.
- One idea per video.
- End with one clear ask: buyers / sellers / investors / stay — pick one.
- Film vertical, natural light, soft background noise is fine (birds in CR, street in Indy = authenticity).
