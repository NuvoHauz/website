# B-roll shot lists

Film these in batches. Name clips on your phone: `indy-banksy-kitchen`, `cr-riu-pool-am`, etc. Vertical 9:16 · 3–8 seconds per clip · slow pans, steady walks, hold stills 2 seconds.

Goal: never scramble for footage when a Reel idea hits.

---

## Indianapolis — Fountain Square

### Banksy House
- Exterior approach (walk-up, full façade)
- Door open / key or code entry (hands only is fine)
- Living room wide → slow push to art / hero wall
- Kitchen island detail (hands placing keys / coffee)
- Bedroom made-bed still + soft window light
- Bathroom clean detail (towel fold, not toilet close-ups)
- Window view / neighborhood tease

### Carriage House
- Same structure as above (exterior → arrival → living → kitchen → sleep → outdoor if any)
- One “difference” clip that makes it feel like its own home (materials, light, layout)

### Neighborhood (non-property)
- Street art / mural walk
- Sidewalk café or porch energy (no filming strangers’ faces without consent)
- Golden hour street
- Downtown skyline from a distance
- Night vs day same corner (powerful for buyer education Reels)

### Agent / you-on-camera B-roll
- Walking a block while talking (friend holds phone)
- Pulling up notes on phone (blur personal info)
- Unlocking a lockbox
- Sitting on steps, talking to camera between showings

## Costa Rica — Paquera / Nicoya

### Riu House (priority — strongest hero)
- Gate / front entrance arrival
- Covered terrace + tropical greens
- Pool wide (shared pool context is fine — still beautiful)
- Glass doors opening to indoor living
- Kitchen island + dining
- Each suite: bed + en-suite vanity glimpse + terrace access
- River / landscape view from terrace
- Outdoor shower detail
- Morning light through living room
- Evening pool lights / warm lamps

### Morpho House & Casita Green Iguana
- Exterior + garden path
- Pool access moment
- Porch coffee / rocking / still life
- Lush detail: leaves, rain on roof, macaw/sky if you’re lucky (don’t chase wildlife)

### Area / lifestyle (makes investors and guests lean in)
- Ferry boarding or bay crossing (wide, safe)
- Paquera road / palms
- Beach cove (honest distance — don’t imply “steps away” if it isn’t)
- Local soda / fresh seafood plate (your hands, your table)
- Sunset over Gulf of Nicoya
- Soft rain on terrace (mood gold)

### Ops B-roll (investor credibility)
- Fresh towels stack
- Restock basket (soap, paper goods)
- Thermostat / AC remote
- Wi‑Fi card or welcome note (branded if you have one)
- Cleaning cart / vacuum pass (honest, not glamorous)
- Calendar on phone (blur guest names)

## Sound banks (record 10–20s)

- Birds / insects morning CR  
- Soft pool water  
- Indy street ambience  
- Door close + footsteps on wood  
- Rain on metal/roof  

Use under VO when music feels fake.

---

## Weekly capture habit

| When | Capture |
|---|---|
| Any showing | 5 exterior + 5 interior clips |
| Turnover day | 3 ops clips |
| CR trip | 20 lifestyle + 20 property clips minimum |
| Good light at home office | 2 talking-head setups for hooks |

---

## What not to film

- Guest faces / kids without written okay  
- Lock codes, wifi passwords, addresses on screen  
- Mess that makes a listing look neglected (unless intentional before/after)  
- Other agents’ listings without permission
