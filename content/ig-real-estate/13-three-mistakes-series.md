# “3 Mistakes” Reels — Sandy Alfaro format

You already use this format (“3 Mistakes first-time…”). Same structure every time:

**Hook (on screen):** “3 mistakes [audience] make in [market]”  
**Body:** Mistake 1 → Mistake 2 → Mistake 3 (5–8 sec each)  
**Close:** “Which one are you making? DM me — I’ll help you fix it before it costs you.”

Film talking-head or voice-over property B-roll. 25–40 seconds total.

---

## M1 — First-time sellers (Indy)

**Hook:** 3 mistakes first-time **sellers** make in Indianapolis

1. **Pricing off the neighbor’s Zestimate** — their house, their updates, their timing. Your price needs your story.  
2. **Listing before the photos match reality** — wide-angle lies bring wrong showings and low offers.  
3. **Being home for every showing** — buyers can’t picture themselves with you in the kitchen.

**CTA:** “Selling this year? DM me — I’ll tell you what to fix first.”

---

## M2 — Indy investors (first rental)

**Hook:** 3 mistakes **Indy investors** make on their first rental

1. **Buying the cheapest house on the block** — cheap often means deferred maintenance with your name on it.  
2. **Running TikTok ADR math** — use slow-month occupancy, not your best weekend.  
3. **Skipping the operator plan** — who turns it, who fixes the HVAC, who handles vacancy?

**CTA:** “First investment? DM me the address you’re looking at.”

---

## M3 — Costa Rica buyers (from the US)

**Hook:** 3 mistakes **Costa Rica buyers** make from Indianapolis

1. **Buying the vacation version** — you need the Tuesday version: rain, internet, access.  
2. **No local team** — attorney, manager, eyes on the ground before you wire money.  
3. **Assuming US timelines** — due diligence and closing don’t always move at Indy speed.

**CTA:** “CR buyers — message me before you get attached to photos.”

---

## M4 — Property tour (buyers)

**Hook:** 3 things I check on every **property tour**

1. **Layout at 9pm** — not just open-house daylight.  
2. **Deferred maintenance hiding in plain sight** — water stains, soft floors, old panels.  
3. **Exit plan** — if you had to sell or rent in 3 years, does this still work?

**CTA:** “Want me on your next tour? DM me — Indy or Costa Rica.”

---

## M5 — Deal analysis (investors)

**Hook:** 3 red flags in a **deal analysis**

1. **Best-case-only rent** — if December kills the deal, it’s not a deal.  
2. **Missing CapEx** — roof, HVAC, appliances don’t last forever.  
3. **No exit** — what if STR rules change or demand softens?

**CTA:** “Send me a deal — I’ll tell you what I’d pressure-test.”

---

## M6 — Renovations (sellers & investors)

**Hook:** 3 **renovation** choices that don’t pay you back

1. **Over-custom for the block** — your taste isn’t always the market’s.  
2. **Pretty before functional** — buyers notice bad layout with new paint.  
3. **Renovating without a sell-or-rent plan** — know who you’re building for.

**CTA:** “Renovating to sell or rent? DM me before you order countertops.”

---

## M7 — First-time buyers (remake / part 2)

**Hook:** 3 more mistakes **first-time buyers** make *(sequel to your existing Reel)*

1. **Skipping the final walk-through** — the house you toured isn’t always the house you get.  
2. **Maxing budget on the payment, not the house** — leave room for repairs and life.  
3. **Falling in love before inspection** — emotion first, offer second.

**CTA:** “First home? DM me FIRST — I’ll send my short pre-offer list.”
