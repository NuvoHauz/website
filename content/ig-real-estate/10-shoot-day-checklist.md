# One shoot day — batch a month of content

**Time:** ~2–3 hours  
**Output:** 8–12 Reels/Shorts worth of raw footage + B-roll bank

---

## Before you leave

- [ ] Charge phone + backup battery  
- [ ] Clean lens  
- [ ] Pick 6 scripts from `02-reels-and-shorts.md` (2 buyer, 2 seller, 2 investor)  
- [ ] One outfit that works in car + indoors + outdoors  
- [ ] Notes app with hooks (glance, don’t read word-for-word)

---

## Block 1 — Talking head (45 min)

Same spot, same light. Film hooks + CTAs separately so you can mix later.

Record each **twice** — second take is usually more natural.

Minimum batch:
- Y1, B1, S1, I1, I2, B3

---

## Block 2 — On-location B-roll (60 min)

At a listing, your investment property (exterior only if occupied), or neighborhood:

From `04-b-roll-shot-lists.md`, get at least:
- 5 exterior / approach clips  
- 5 interior walk-through clips (permission only)  
- 3 detail clips (kitchen, yard, street)  
- 2 day-vs-evening if possible  

Hold each shot 3 full seconds. Slow pans only.

---

## Block 3 — Car / between appointments (15 min)

Best for hot takes and 15-second hooks.  
Film 5 hooks from the batch list in `02-reels-and-shorts.md`.

---

## Same-day edit rule

Post **one** Reel within 24 hours — even imperfect.  
Momentum beats polish.

Suggested first post: **Y1** with caption from `06-hooks-captions-ctas.md`.

---

## File naming on phone

`2026-08-sandy-y1-take2.mp4`  
`2026-08-indy-kitchen-pan.mp4`  
`2026-08-cr-gate-arrival.mp4`

You’ll thank yourself in week 3.

---

## What “done” looks like

- [ ] 6+ talking-head clips  
- [ ] 15+ B-roll clips  
- [ ] 1 Reel posted  
- [ ] 3 Stories from the shoot (“filming content for buyers/sellers/investors — what do you want to see?”)
