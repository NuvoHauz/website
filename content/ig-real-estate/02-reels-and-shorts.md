# Reels & Shorts — scripts for lead gen

**20–35 seconds.** Hook → one point → one CTA.  
Rewrite any line that doesn’t sound like your mouth.

Brackets = swap with your real numbers/stories.

---

## BUYERS

### B1 — Shop with investor eyes (even if it’s your home)
**Hook:** “I don’t tour houses like a normal buyer.”  
**Body:** “I’m looking at layout, maintenance, and whether this still works if life changes in three years — keep it, rent it, or sell it.”  
**Visual:** Walking a listing (hallway → kitchen → yard). Face to camera for the last line.  
**CTA:** “Buyers in Indy or looking at Costa Rica — DM me what you’re trying to solve.”

### B2 — Fountain Square / Indy neighborhood honesty
**Hook:** “Don’t buy a neighborhood from daytime photos.”  
**Body:** “Walk it at night. Sit in traffic at the hour you’d actually commute. Listen for noise. Pretty listings lie by omission.”  
**Visual:** Street walk day vs dusk (same block if you can).  
**CTA:** “Tell me your non‑negotiables. I’ll tell you which Indy areas fit.”

### B3 — Costa Rica buyers: pretty vs. ownable
**Hook:** “Buying in Costa Rica isn’t the hard part. Owning well is.”  
**Body:** “Verify water, internet at the exact pin, road access in rainy season, and who handles the property when you’re not in the country.”  
**Visual:** Drive + gate + exterior. You on camera for the checklist.  
**CTA:** “Serious about Costa Rica? Message me — I’ll share what I’d verify before you move money.”

### B4 — First-time buyer, straight talk
**Hook:** “First home? Ignore the paint.”  
**Body:** “Roof, mechanicals, layout, location. Paint is a weekend. Bad bones are a five-year tax.”  
**CTA:** “DM me FIRST and I’ll send the short list I make buyers check before they get emotional.”

### B5 — What I’d ask before you write an offer
**Hook:** “Before you offer, ask me these three.”  
**Body:** “1) Why is the seller moving? 2) What’s been deferred? 3) Who else wants this house — and at what price reality?”  
**CTA:** “Under contract anxiety hits everyone. Buyers — talk to me before you guess.”

## SELLERS

### S1 — Prep that actually helps
**Hook:** “Staging isn’t decorating for fun. It’s removing friction.”  
**Body:** “Clear counters, soft light, less stuff, one strong first photo. Buyers decide in seconds whether they feel calm or crowded.”  
**Visual:** Before/after counter or living room.  
**CTA:** “Selling soon? Send me 5 phone photos — I’ll tell you what’s helping and what’s hurting.”

### S2 — Pricing without the pep talk
**Hook:** “Overpricing isn’t confidence. It’s a longer listing.”  
**Body:** “I’d rather put you at the number that brings the right buyers early than watch you chase the market down.”  
**CTA:** “Sellers who want a straight conversation — DM me.”

### S3 — What kills showings
**Hook:** “Your house can be fine and still show badly.”  
**Body:** “Smell, clutter, dark rooms, deferred fixes in photos. Fix the first impression before you blame the market.”  
**CTA:** “Pre-list walkthroughs — message me if you want eyes on it before it hits MLS.”

### S4 — Timing the list
**Hook:** “When to list depends on your next move — not a slogan.”  
**Body:** “If you need to buy after, we plan the chain. If you’re done with the house, we price for action. Same inventory, different strategy.”  
**CTA:** “Tell me your timeline. I’ll tell you the cleaner path.”

## INVESTORS

### I1 — Your wedge (use weekly)
**Hook:** “I’m not a guru. I have properties.”  
**Body:** “Indianapolis and Costa Rica. Agent work and ownership. I care about the deal after the closing — because I live with those decisions.”  
**Visual:** Keys / exterior / you talking.  
**CTA:** “Investors — DM me Indy or Costa Rica and what you’re trying to build.”

### I2 — Indy vs Costa Rica (no fantasy)
**Hook:** “These are not the same investment.”  
**Body:** “Indy: closer management, clearer comps, different demand. Costa Rica: lifestyle + tourism dynamics, more logistics, you need local partnership. Choose the problem you want.”  
**CTA:** “Comment INDY or CR — I’ll reply with the first five questions I’d ask in that market.”

### I3 — Kill the TikTok math
**Hook:** “If the deal only works on best-case rent, it doesn’t work.”  
**Body:** “I underwrite slow months, real expenses, CapEx, and who operates it. Spreadsheets don’t change lightbulbs.”  
**CTA:** “Send an address you’re considering. I’ll tell you what I’d pressure-test.”

### I4 — Operator truth (your Airbnb ownership = credibility only)
**Hook:** “Owning a rental taught me more than listing photos ever did.”  
**Body:** “Turnover, maintenance, vacancy, local rules — that’s the real P&L. If you hate operations, buy a different structure or hire it out on purpose.”  
**CTA:** “Want operator-level advice, not hype? Investors — message me.”

### I5 — What I won’t help you chase
**Hook:** “I pass on ‘too good’ deals.”  
**Body:** “No clear path to manage it. No honest expense line. No exit if rules change. That’s not discipline — that’s protecting your downside.”  
**CTA:** “If you want a partner who’ll say no, DM me.”

## YOU / TRUST (still lead-gen)

### Y1 — Who I work with
**Hook:** “I work with three kinds of people.”  
**Body:** “Buyers who want clarity. Sellers who want honesty. Investors who want numbers that survive contact with reality.”  
**CTA:** “Which one are you? Reply buy, sell, or invest.”

### Y2 — Two-country brain
**Hook:** “People ask how I work Indy and Costa Rica.”  
**Body:** “Same standard: verify, don’t romanticize. Different due diligence checklist. I don’t copy-paste strategy across borders.”  
**CTA:** “Cross-border or local — tell me your situation.”

### Y3 — Soft authority
**Hook:** “I don’t need every client.”  
**Body:** “I’d rather take the ones who want straight answers. That keeps the work clean — for you and for me.”  
**CTA:** “If that sounds like you, introduce yourself in the DMs.”
