# Your IG voice

## Who you are in the bio / on camera

Sandy — agent and investor working **Indianapolis** and **Costa Rica**.  
You don’t talk like a corporate listing machine. You talk like someone who’s bought, held, and operated property — and helps clients do the same without the fantasy math.

## Your edge (use this, don’t soft-pedal it)

Most agents only sell.  
Most “investors” on IG only teach.  
You do both markets and you own investment property in both — so your advice comes from decisions you actually made.

Airbnb/hosting experience = **operator credibility for investors and investor-minded buyers**.  
It is not content about check-in times, guest reviews, or “book direct.”

## Sound like this

| Cut this | Say this |
|---|---|
| “Dream home alert 🚨” | “Here’s who this house is actually for — and who should keep driving.” |
| “Passive income unlocked!” | “This only works if you count cleaning, vacancy, and your time.” |
| “The market is HOT!!!” | “Here’s what buyers are asking me in Indy this week.” |
| “DM me READY” | “If you’re buying, selling, or investing — tell me which one.” |
| “As a Realtor®…” | “I’ve bought here and I advise clients here — same questions apply.” |

## Content pillars (rotate these)

1. **Buyers** — neighborhoods, what to inspect, Indy vs CR realities  
2. **Sellers** — pricing honesty, prep that actually moves the needle  
3. **Investors** — underwriting, Indy vs Costa Rica, operator truths  
4. **You** — face-to-camera takes, day-in-the-work, opinion posts (builds trust)

Skip: vacation vibes as the main message, guest-facing tours, “escape to paradise” ads.

## Filming habits

- Hook in the first second  
- One idea per Reel  
- One audience per CTA (buyer **or** seller **or** investor)  
- Vertical, natural light, talking to one smart person — not a crowd  
- If a line could be any agent in any city, delete it and add Indy or Costa Rica specificity

## Anti-AI checklist (run before every post)

- [ ] Did I use a **specific place** (Fountain Square, Paquera, Nicoya — not “Costa Rica paradise”)?  
- [ ] Did I admit a **tradeoff** (not everything is amazing)?  
- [ ] Is there **one opinion** someone might disagree with?  
- [ ] Did I cut words like: unlock, game-changer, journey, elevate, curated lifestyle?  
- [ ] Would I say this on a showing or investor call without cringing?  
- [ ] Is the CTA **one clear action** — not “follow for more tips!!!”?
