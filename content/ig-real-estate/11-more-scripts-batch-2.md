# Reels batch 2 — more human scripts

Same rules: hook fast, one idea, one CTA. Say it like you’re talking to one person.

---

## BUYERS

### B6 — The open house trap
**Hook:** “Open houses are for browsing — not deciding.”  
**Body:** “Use them to learn a neighborhood. Don’t fall in love with staging and ignore the inspection, the street, and your actual commute.”  
**CTA:** “Buyers — DM me the areas you’re circling. I’ll tell you what to verify before you get attached.”

### B7 — Costa Rica from the US
**Hook:** “Buying Costa Rica from Indianapolis? Same mistake every time.”  
**Body:** “People buy the vacation version of the property. I want you to buy the Tuesday version — rain, internet, access, who fixes the AC.”  
**CTA:** “CR buyers — message me before you wire earnest money.”

### B8 — When to walk away
**Hook:** “I’ll tell you to walk away.”  
**Body:** “If the numbers only work if nothing breaks for five years, if the seller won’t disclose, or if you’re buying panic — walk. There’s always another house.”  
**CTA:** “Need a second opinion on a deal? DM me.”

## SELLERS

### S5 — The neighbor sold for…
**Hook:** “Your neighbor’s sale price isn’t your price.”  
**Body:** “Condition, layout, updates, timing, and buyer pool all differ. Copy-paste pricing is how listings sit.”  
**CTA:** “Thinking of selling? DM me your address — I’ll talk straight about positioning.”

### S6 — Photos lie (sometimes)
**Hook:** “Wide-angle saved this listing. And hurt the seller.”  
**Body:** “Buyers show up expecting space you don’t have. Better to show honest rooms that match reality — fewer wasted showings.”  
**CTA:** “Sellers — send listing photos. I’ll tell you if they’re helping.”

### S7 — Emotional equity
**Hook:** “You’re not wrong to love your house.”  
**Body:** “You’re wrong if you expect the market to pay for every memory. Price for the buyer who’s comparing four other options this weekend.”  
**CTA:** “Hard conversation? That’s what I’m for. DM me.”

## INVESTORS

### I6 — House hack vs. real portfolio
**Hook:** “Your first rental isn’t a portfolio.”  
**Body:** “It’s a test. Can you handle vacancy, a $4k HVAC, a bad tenant month? If yes, we talk deal two. If no, we fix the structure first.”  
**CTA:** “First investment? DM me Indy or CR.”

### I7 — Costa Rica partnership
**Hook:** “I don’t buy Costa Rica alone.”  
**Body:** “Local attorney, local manager, local eyes on the ground. If your plan is ‘figure it out from Indiana,’ we need to talk before you buy.”  
**CTA:** “CR investors — DM me your plan. I’ll stress-test it.”

### I8 — STR vs long-term (Indy)
**Hook:** “Not every Indy house should be a short-term rental.”  
**Body:** “Rules, neighbors, furniture cycle, turnover — sometimes long-term is the smarter play. The goal is cash flow you can live with, not a category label.”  
**CTA:** “Got an address? Send it. I’ll tell you what I’d actually do with it.”

## YOU — personality posts (high trust)

### Y4 — Why I’m on IG
**Hook:** “I’m not here to post sold signs.”  
**Body:** “I’m here because people get bad advice dressed up as confidence — especially cross-border. If I can save you one expensive mistake, this account paid for itself.”  
**CTA:** “Follow if you want straight talk. DM if you’re ready to move.”

### Y5 — Unpopular opinion
**Hook:** “Unpopular opinion: most people shouldn’t invest out of state yet.”  
**Body:** “If you haven’t owned something local, learned a repair cycle, and sat through a slow month — going remote adds complexity you’re not ready to pay for.”  
**CTA:** “Disagree? Comment. Curious? DM me your situation.”

### Y6 — Day in the life (montage, minimal VO)
**Visual:** Showings, calls, driving, notes, CR/Indy context clips.  
**VO (end only):** “Agent by day. Investor always. Two markets. One rule: tell the truth.”  
**CTA:** “Buy, sell, or invest — say which.”
