# B-roll for your personal IG

Bank footage while you work. This supports **agent + investor** content — not vacation ads.

Vertical 9:16 · 3–8 sec clips · name files clearly (`indy-showing-kitchen`, `cr-gate-arrival`).

---

## Indianapolis

### On the job
- Walking up to a listing (exterior approach)  
- Lockbox / door open (hands only)  
- Room-to-room walk: entry → living → kitchen → yard  
- Notepad or phone notes (blur private info)  
- Pulling up to a curb, stepping out  
- Sitting in the car between showings (talking-head B-roll)  
- Handshake-free “leaving a property” walk-away  

### Neighborhood texture (buyer education)
- Same block day vs evening  
- Street context, parks, local corridors you actually recommend  
- Skyline / downtown only if it supports the point  

### Seller-prep B-roll
- Clearing a counter  
- Opening curtains / blinds  
- Made bed still  
- Before/after of one room (huge for carousels + Reels)

---

## Costa Rica (investment / buying education)

### Property diligence visuals
- Gate / road access  
- Exterior and site drainage hints  
- Terrace / outdoor living (as *asset quality*, not vacation promo)  
- Drive to the pin  
- Rainy-day access (gold for honest CR content)

### Lifestyle context (use sparingly — always tied to a teaching point)
- Ferry or travel logistics (for “owning from abroad” posts)  
- Local town / services proximity  
- Sunset only if the VO is about market/lifestyle demand — not “book a trip”

### Operator credibility (investment angle)
- Maintenance callout (AC, roof detail, exterior wear)  
- Supply run / vendor meetup (no guest faces)  
- Keys, lockbox, management notebook  

Do **not** film guest check-ins, welcome books, or “rate this stay” content on this account.

---

## Face-to-camera setups (film once, reuse hooks)

1. Soft indoor light, plain background — education hooks  
2. In-car between appointments — hot takes  
3. Exterior of a property behind you — market/neighborhood posts  

Record 8–10 hooks in one outfit/session.

---

## Sound banks

- Soft Indy street ambience  
- CR rain / birds (under VO for diligence posts)  
- Door close + footsteps  

---

## Never put on screen

- Lock codes, addresses of private clients, contracts  
- Guest faces or unreleased off-market addresses without permission  
- Another agent’s listing as if it’s yours
