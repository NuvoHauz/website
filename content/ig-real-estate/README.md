## Goal of every post

Someone who might buy, sell, or invest **DMs you** or saves your content to message later.

Not: likes from other agents. Not: booking inquiries for vacation stays.

## The human test

Before you post, read the caption out loud. If it sounds like a ChatGPT realtor carousel, rewrite one sentence with something only you would say — a place name, a mistake you’ve seen, a line you use with clients.
