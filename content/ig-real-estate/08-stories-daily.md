# Instagram Stories — daily lead-gen (5 min/day)

Stories keep you top of mind between Reels. Keep them **client-facing**, not vacation-hosting.

---

## Monday — “What I’m working on”

**Format:** selfie or car clip  
**Text:** “This week I’m helping [buyers / sellers / investors] in [Indy / Costa Rica]. If that’s you — reply to this story.”  
**Sticker:** poll — Buy / Sell / Invest

---

## Tuesday — Quick tip (one slide)

Rotate these:

- Buyers: “Before you offer: walk the block at night.”  
- Sellers: “Your first photo matters more than your description.”  
- Investors: “If the deal only works on best-case rent, pass.”

**CTA sticker:** “Questions? DM me.”

---

## Wednesday — Q&A box

**Prompt:** “Ask me anything about buying, selling, or investing in Indy or Costa Rica.”  
Answer 2–3 honestly on video. Skip questions you can’t answer without a consult — say “DM me the address / timeline and I’ll go deeper.”

---

## Thursday — B-roll + text

Silent clip: walking a listing, street, or CR road.  
**Text overlay:** one line from `02-reels-and-shorts.md` 15-second hooks.  
**Link sticker:** DM or calendar if you use one.

---

## Friday — “This week I said no to…”

Builds trust with investors and sellers.

Examples:
- “A deal that only worked on fantasy occupancy.”  
- “A list price the seller loved but the market wouldn’t pay.”  
- “A CR property with no clear management plan.”

**CTA:** “Want someone who’ll tell you the truth? DM me.”

---

## Weekend — Personal + soft CTA

Coffee, travel between markets, family moment (your comfort level).  
One line: “Two markets, one standard — straight answers.”  
Optional: “Who’s exploring Indy or Costa Rica real estate right now?”

---

## Story highlight buckets (create these)

| Highlight | Cover label | Save these stories |
|---|---|---|
| Buy | BUY | buyer tips, neighborhood walks, checklists |
| Sell | SELL | prep tips, pricing honesty, before/after |
| Invest | INVEST | underwriting, Indy vs CR, operator truths |
| About | ME | intro Reels, who you help, how to DM |
| CR | CR | Costa Rica diligence only — not vacation promos |
| Indy | INDY | Indianapolis buyer/seller content |

Pin **About** or **Buy** as the first highlight visitors see.
