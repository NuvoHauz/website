## Anti-AI checklist (before every post)

- [ ] Specific place named (Fountain Square, Paquera, Nicoya — not “paradise”)
- [ ] One honest tradeoff admitted
- [ ] One opinion someone might disagree with
- [ ] Cut: unlock, game-changer, journey, elevate, curated lifestyle
- [ ] Would Sandy say this on a showing without cringing?
- [ ] One clear CTA — not “follow for more tips!!!”
