## Every Reel structure

**Hook → Value → CTA** · **20–45 seconds**

1. **Hook** (0–3 sec) — curiosity, pattern interrupt, make them stop scrolling  
2. **Value** — one clear takeaway from real experience (investor, host, broker)  
3. **CTA** — one ask: DM, comment, send address, etc.

## Content focus (weight heavily)

- Indianapolis neighborhoods — **Fountain Square**, downtown, near-downtown
- **Investing** — rentals, STR, underwriting, slow-month math
- **Renovations** — what paid back, what didn't, real numbers tone
- **Airbnb** — operator truth (turnover, guests, reviews) → investor credibility, **not** guest booking ads
- **Real life** — keys, showings, reno mess, laundry, lockbox moments

## Hooks that stop the scroll

- Start with tension: "Stop—before you offer on a Fountain Square house…"
- Call out a mistake: "I see investors do this in Indy every week."
- Specific place: "Fountain Square at 9pm looks different than noon."
- Contrarian: "I'd pass on this house—and here's why."
- Personal: "I lost money on this renovation choice."

Avoid: questions with obvious yes answers, "3 things you need to know," emoji walls.

## Bilingual

When the topic fits Indy buyers/sellers/investors who speak Spanish → include **Español** version below English. Same Hook → Value → CTA. Natural spoken Spanish, not formal.

## Anti-generic-Realtor list

Never lead with:
- "I'm so excited to announce…"
- "Just listed!" (unless paired with a real lesson)
- "Don't miss this opportunity"
- "DM me READY / LINK IN BIO"
- Passive income / unlock / game-changer / journey
