## C1 — Before you fall in love with a house (Buyers · Indy)

1. **Cover:** 6 things I check BEFORE my buyers fall in love with a house  
2. **Will the layout still work in 5 years?** Not just today.  
3. **Come back after 5 PM.** Traffic, parking, and noise feel different.  
4. **Pretty paint can hide deferred stuff.** Look past staging.  
5. **Could you sell or rent this later?** If life changes.  
6. **Your mortgage isn't your only monthly expense.** Budget real life.  
7. **CTA:** Save this before your next showing. Buying in Indy? DM me "BUYER."
