# Hook Bank

Grab a hook → find full script in `03 REELS/To-Record/` or expand in `02 CONTENT IDEAS/`.

## Hook formulas

1. **Contrarian local:** “Don’t buy a neighborhood from daytime photos.”
2. **Operator truth:** “If it only works on best-case rent, it doesn’t work.”
3. **Identity:** “I’m an agent and an investor — those jobs argue until the answer gets better.”
4. **Place-specific:** “Buying in Costa Rica isn’t hard. Owning well is.”
5. **Offer:** “Send me 5 phone photos…”
6. **Filter:** “If you want hype, keep scrolling.”
7. **3 Mistakes:** “3 mistakes [audience] make in [market]” ← Sandy’s proven format
