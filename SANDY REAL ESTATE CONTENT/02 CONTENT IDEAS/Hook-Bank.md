# Hook Bank — curiosity hooks (Indianapolis)

Use for Reels, Stories, on-screen text. Pair with full script in `Spoken-Reels-Indy.md`.

**Format every time:** Hook → Value → CTA

---

## Stop-scroll hooks (Indy / Fountain Square)

1. "Everyone loves Fountain Square at lunch. Almost nobody walks it at nine."
2. "Would I put this house on Airbnb? Maybe. Probably not."
3. "This wide-angle photo got you showings. It also got you low offers."
4. "Your neighbor sold for what? Cool. That is not your price."
5. "Nobody posts turnover day. That's the actual Airbnb business."
6. "This house photographs beautiful. The electrical panel tells a different story."
7. "Mass Ave and Fountain Square are fifteen minutes apart. Different math."
8. "If the deal only works when every weekend is booked, I pass."
9. "Fountain Square is not for everyone—and I mean that as a compliment."
10. "Guest check-in Saturday. HVAC died Friday night. That's investing."

---

## Investor / Airbnb operator

1. "The listing photo is the easy part. Turnover day is the business."
2. "This is a great long-term rental. It would be a terrible Airbnb."
3. "I spent money on a renovation that did not come back to me."
4. "Spreadsheets don't change locks at midnight."
5. "Three mistakes I see Indy investors make—and I almost made number two."

## Seller

1. "You're not staging for Pinterest. You're staging so a stranger can breathe."
2. "I'd rather you sell in three weeks at the right number than flex for two months."
3. "Price for the buyer shopping this Sunday—not the story you want."

## Buyer (avoid first-time buyer mistakes angle — posted)

3. "Pretty kitchens don't fix bad flow."
4. "The inspection is not optional just because you love the exposed brick."
