## Deal analysis content

- Occupancy: defend slow month, not best weekend
- All-in expenses: cleaning, software, supplies, maintenance, **your time**
- CapEx: roof, HVAC, appliances, furniture cycle
- Exit: rules change, demand softens — then what?
- CTA: “Send an address — I’ll pressure-test it”
