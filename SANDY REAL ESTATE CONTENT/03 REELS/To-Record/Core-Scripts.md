# Core Reel Scripts

**Format:** Hook → Body → CTA · 20–35 seconds

---

## BUYERS

### B1 — Shop with investor eyes
**Hook:** “I don’t tour houses like a normal buyer.”  
**Body:** “I’m looking at layout, maintenance, and whether this still works if life changes in three years — keep it, rent it, or sell it.”  
**Visual:** Listing walk-through. Face to camera at end.  
**CTA:** “Buyers in Indy or Costa Rica — DM me what you’re trying to solve.”

### B2 — Indy neighborhood honesty
**Hook:** “Don’t buy a neighborhood from daytime photos.”  
**Body:** “Walk it at night. Sit in traffic at the hour you’d actually commute. Listen for noise.”  
**CTA:** “Tell me your non‑negotiables. I’ll tell you which Indy areas fit.”

### B3 — Costa Rica: pretty vs ownable
**Hook:** “Buying in Costa Rica isn’t the hard part. Owning well is.”  
**Body:** “Verify water, internet at the exact pin, road access in rainy season, and who handles the property when you’re not in the country.”  
**CTA:** “Serious about Costa Rica? Message me before you move money.”

### B4 — First-time buyer
**Hook:** “First home? Ignore the paint.”  
**Body:** “Roof, mechanicals, layout, location. Paint is a weekend. Bad bones are a five-year tax.”  
**CTA:** “DM me FIRST — I’ll send my pre-offer checklist.”

### B5 — Before you offer
**Hook:** “Before you offer, ask me these three.”  
**Body:** “Why is the seller moving? What’s been deferred? Who else wants this house — at what price reality?”  
**CTA:** “Buyers — talk to me before you guess.”

## SELLERS

### S1 — Prep that helps
**Hook:** “Staging isn’t decorating for fun. It’s removing friction.”  
**Body:** “Clear counters, soft light, less stuff, one strong first photo.”  
**CTA:** “Selling soon? Send me 5 phone photos.”

### S2 — Pricing honesty
**Hook:** “Overpricing isn’t confidence. It’s a longer listing.”  
**Body:** “I’d rather price for the right buyers in week one than chase the market down.”  
**CTA:** “Sellers who want a straight conversation — DM me.”

### S3 — What kills showings
**Hook:** “Your house can be fine and still show badly.”  
**Body:** “Smell, clutter, dark rooms, deferred fixes in photos.”  
**CTA:** “Pre-list walkthrough — message me before MLS.”

### S4 — Timing the list
**Hook:** “When to list depends on your next move — not a slogan.”  
**Body:** “Buy-after chain vs price-for-action — same inventory, different strategy.”  
**CTA:** “Tell me your timeline.”

## INVESTORS

### I1 — Your wedge
**Hook:** “I’m not a guru. I have properties.”  
**Body:** “Indianapolis and Costa Rica. Agent work and ownership. I care about the deal after closing.”  
**CTA:** “Investors — DM me Indy or Costa Rica.”

### I2 — Indy vs Costa Rica
**Hook:** “These are not the same investment.”  
**Body:** “Indy: closer ops, clearer comps. Costa Rica: more logistics, need local partnership. Choose the problem you want.”  
**CTA:** “Comment INDY or CR.”

### I3 — Kill TikTok math
**Hook:** “If the deal only works on best-case rent, it doesn’t work.”  
**Body:** “Slow months, real expenses, CapEx, who operates it.”  
**CTA:** “Send an address — I’ll pressure-test it.”

### I4 — Operator truth
**Hook:** “Owning a rental taught me more than listing photos ever did.”  
**Body:** “Turnover, maintenance, vacancy, local rules — the real P&L.”  
**CTA:** “Want operator-level advice? Message me.”

### I5 — What I won’t chase
**Hook:** “I pass on ‘too good’ deals.”  
**Body:** “No management plan. No honest expenses. No exit if rules change.”  
**CTA:** “Want a partner who’ll say no? DM me.”
