# Spoken Reels — Indianapolis (English + Español)

**Style guide:** [`Sandy-Content-Style-Guide.md`](../../01 BRAND & VOICE/Sandy-Content-Style-Guide.md)

**Structure:** HOOK → VALUE → PAYOFF → CTA · **20–45 sec**  
**Voice:** conversational — read out loud. If it sounds like a brochure, rewrite.  
**Markets:** Indianapolis first. Costa Rica only when Sandy asks to combine.  
**Skip posted topics:** first-time buyer mistakes *(on grid)*

Film in order of ⭐ priority.

---

## R01 — Fountain Square at 9pm ⭐ BUYERS

**~35 sec · Fountain Square · B-roll: same block day vs night**

### English
**Hook:** "Everyone loves Fountain Square at lunch. Almost nobody walks it at nine."

**Value:** "Before you write an offer down here, come back at night. Same block. Same house. Listen for street noise, check parking, see how it feels when you're tired—not when you're brunching on Virginia Ave. I do this on every serious buyer tour."

**CTA:** "Buying near downtown? DM me the streets you're looking at. I'll tell you what to check before you fall in love."

### Español
**Hook:** "A todos les encanta Fountain Square a mediodía. Casi nadie lo camina a las nueve de la noche."

**Value:** "Antes de hacer una oferta, vuelve de noche. Mismo cuadro. Misma casa. Escucha el ruido, mira el estacionamiento, siente cómo se vive cuando estás cansado—no cuando estás desayunando. Yo hago esto en cada tour serio."

**CTA:** "¿Comprando cerca del downtown? Escríbeme qué calles miras y te digo qué revisar antes de enamorarte."
